# EU Compliance Watch — Featured Image Prompt Standards

## Shared visual direction

Create a clean editorial illustration for a European civic technology and public accountability publication. Show evidence trails, public-service environments, documents, interfaces and accountable human decision-makers. Use restrained institutional colours, high visual clarity and a horizontal 16:9 composition. Avoid humanoid robots, glowing brains, cyberpunk cityscapes, dominant flags and official institutional logos.

## Deep Dive

A layered civic oversight scene showing a citizen, a public institution and a transparent evidence chain connecting rules, records, human review and accountability. Sophisticated editorial illustration, navy and EU-blue emphasis, generous negative space for title placement.

## Watch Report

A regulatory alert desk with a newly issued document, timeline markers and highlighted evidence gaps. Editorial news-analysis style, navy and cyan emphasis, clear focal point, no sensationalism.

## Citizen Guide

A citizen following a practical step-by-step path through a public-service process, with clear documents, questions and escalation routes. Accessible and constructive editorial style, navy and green emphasis.

## Citizen Checklist

A compact field-tool concept with checklist, evidence folder, timestamp, screenshot and verification status. Precise infographic-inspired illustration, navy and violet emphasis.

## Prohibited visual shortcuts

- generic robot heads;
- binary rain or hacker imagery;
- EU institutional insignia;
- courtroom gavels unless legally necessary;
- opaque black boxes without an evidence context;
- text-heavy generated images.

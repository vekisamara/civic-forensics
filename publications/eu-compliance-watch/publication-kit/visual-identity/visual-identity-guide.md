# EU Compliance Watch — Visual Identity Guide v3.0

## 1. Identity

**Master brand:** Civic Forensics  
**Publication line:** EU Compliance Watch  
**Series labels:** Deep Dive, Watch Report, Citizen Guide, Citizen Checklist

The identity should communicate evidence, regulatory scrutiny, public accountability and European relevance without imitating an EU institution or suggesting institutional endorsement.

## 2. Design principles

- restrained and evidence-led;
- high contrast and accessible;
- editorial rather than promotional;
- repeatable across Word, PDF, portal and social media;
- clear distinction between legal fact, analysis, warning and recommendation.

## 3. Typography

Preferred open/system-safe stack:

- Headings: `Aptos Display`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Body: `Aptos`, `Arial`, sans-serif
- Legal references and metadata: `IBM Plex Mono`, `Consolas`, monospace

Minimum body size: 10.5 pt in print and 17 px on the web. Use sentence case for headings. Do not use full paragraphs in uppercase.

## 4. Colour roles

- Navy — institutional base and headings
- EU blue — series identifier and links
- Cyan — evidence and information
- Amber — watchpoint and incomplete verification
- Red — red flag and likely non-compliance
- Green — good practice and verified compliance
- Violet — methodology and evidence requirements
- Off-white — document background

Exact values are defined in `color-palette.json`.

## 5. Series identifiers

- Deep Dive: navy + EU blue
- Watch Report: navy + cyan
- Citizen Guide: navy + green
- Citizen Checklist: navy + violet

Document IDs must remain visible on the cover, footer and citation page.

## 6. Standard callout boxes

- **Civic Forensics Quick Verdict** — concise status assessment
- **Key Finding** — central supported finding
- **Evidence Required** — documents or records needed for verification
- **Red Flag** — warning pattern
- **Good Practice** — observable compliance measure
- **Watchpoint** — development requiring monitoring
- **Questions to Ask** — operational oversight questions

Each box must use both a text label and a colour cue; colour alone must never carry meaning.

## 7. Cover standard

Required elements:

1. CIVIC FORENSICS
2. EU COMPLIANCE WATCH
3. Series type
4. Document ID
5. Title and optional subtitle
6. Version and date
7. Language
8. Abstract geometric or civic-tech illustration

Do not use the EU flag as the dominant visual. Do not reproduce EU institutional logos.

## 8. Images

Preferred style: clean editorial illustration, evidence layers, public-service context, document trails, audit interfaces and human accountability. Avoid humanoid robots, glowing brains and generic cyberpunk imagery.

## 9. Accessibility

- target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast;
- provide alt text for every meaningful image;
- never encode status using colour only;
- preserve heading hierarchy;
- use real lists and tables;
- ensure reading order survives PDF export.

## 10. Footer

`Civic Forensics | EU Compliance Watch | [DOCUMENT ID] | [VERSION]`

Page number appears on the outer edge.

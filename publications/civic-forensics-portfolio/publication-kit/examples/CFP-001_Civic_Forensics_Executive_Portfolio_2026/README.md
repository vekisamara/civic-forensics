The portfolio is designed as a universal first-contact document for international institutions, donors, civil society, research and policy partners.

## Publication workflow

1. Complete the evidence register.
2. Verify all portal, publication and repository links.
3. Add official contact details.
4. Confirm bibliographic data for the field manual and other publications.
5. Conduct editorial, accessibility and legal-risk review.
6. Convert the canonical Markdown source into the standardised Word edition.
7. Produce the final PDF and portal summary.
8. Change the publication version from `0.1` to `1.0` and status to `Published — Current`.

## Status discipline

Every asset must be labelled as one of:

- Published output;
- Operational asset;
- Prototype;
- Concept;
- Planned.

Future pilot targets must never be presented as completed achievements.

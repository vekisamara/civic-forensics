## Verification rule

No claim should enter the “Current Achievements” section of CFP-001 without a completed evidence field and a clear status label.

## Final-publication blockers

- official contact email;
- stable publication URL;
- final portal and repository links for ECW-DD-001;
- bibliographic record for the field manual / book;
- current analytics, where reach or use is claimed;
- final review of public case-study references;
- stable release or merged status for ECW and CFP publication kits;
- editorial, accessibility and legal-risk review of the formatted edition.

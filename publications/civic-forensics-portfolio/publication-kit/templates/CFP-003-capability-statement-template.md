## Identity

Civic Forensics is an Independent Civic Intelligence Initiative developing practical methods, publications and AI-assisted tools for public accountability, democratic resilience and civic oversight.

## Core Capabilities

### Regulatory and institutional analysis

- EU compliance monitoring;
- analysis of public administration and official responses;
- transparency and access-to-information workflows;
- public-money and public-service scrutiny.

### AI literacy and civic education

- practical AI literacy for citizens and civil society;
- safe use of AI in documentary and administrative analysis;
- citizen guides, checklists and training materials.

### Evidence and publication systems

- Civic Forensics methodology;
- EU Compliance Watch;
- versioned publication standards;
- evidence matrices, case studies and audit-oriented outputs.

### Civic technology

- open prompt library;
- Civic Intelligence Dashboard concept and prototypes;
- structured evidence, claim and case schemas;
- open repository and reusable resources.

## The challenge

Public institutions increasingly use digital systems and AI while citizens often lack practical means to verify transparency, responsibility and compliance. Administrative formalism can satisfy procedural appearance without producing meaningful accountability.

## The response

Civic Forensics combines:

- evidence-based civic oversight;
- AI literacy;
- regulatory analysis;
- open methodologies;
- practical citizen tools;
- versioned publications;
- civic-technology concepts and prototypes.

## Current assets

- Civic Forensics portals;
- open GitHub methodology and prompt library;
- Democratic Resilience & AI Literacy Program;
- EU Compliance Watch publication system;
- case studies and civic oversight workflows;
- Civic Intelligence Dashboard concept and prototypes.

## Proposed next step

A short exploratory meeting to present the initiative, receive technical feedback and identify a realistic cooperation pathway such as policy dialogue, expert exchange, a workshop or a pilot.

## Relationship to ECW

ECW uses navy and EU blue for regulatory monitoring. CFP uses deep teal and restrained gold for institutional, strategic and partnership documents. Typography, accessibility and evidence-oriented layout remain part of one Civic Forensics publication family.

## Cover standard

A cover should contain only:

- Civic Forensics name;
- institutional descriptor;
- document type;
- title;
- document ID;
- publication year and version;
- one simple visual or geometric field.

Avoid dense collages, decorative EU flags, partner logos without permission and unsupported claims.

## Typography

Use widely available fonts:

- Headings: Aptos Display, Arial or Liberation Sans Bold.
- Body: Aptos, Arial or Liberation Sans.
- Legal and technical identifiers: a consistent sans-serif font with increased letter spacing.

## Callout system

- **Strategic Opportunity** — teal label and text heading.
- **Current Achievement** — green label and evidence reference.
- **Future Development** — amber label and explicit status.
- **Partnership Opportunity** — civic blue label.
- **Key Impact** — deep teal label.
- **Risk or Limitation** — red label.
- **Evidence of Capacity** — green label with source path or link.

## Image rules

Images must add evidentiary, explanatory or navigational value. AI-assisted illustrations require disclosure in the publication information. Every meaningful image requires alt text.

## Accessibility

- minimum body size equivalent to 10.5 pt in print;
- sufficient contrast;
- real heading styles;
- meaningful reading order;
- no information communicated by colour alone;
- descriptive links and alt text;
- accessible tables with header rows.

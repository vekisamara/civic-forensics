# Civic Forensics

**Independent Civic Intelligence Initiative**

Building democratic resilience through evidence, technology and civic intelligence.

Civic Forensics is the international English-language repository for EU compliance monitoring, AI literacy, evidence-based public accountability, civic-intelligence methods and publication systems.

## Repository scope

- EU Compliance Watch
- Civic Forensics Portfolio
- Democratic Resilience & AI Literacy Program
- English-language methodology and citizen tools
- Research concepts and publication infrastructure

## Repository structure

- [`analyses/`](analyses/README.md) — canonical English-language analytical sources, templates and source registers.
- [`blog/`](blog/) — WordPress-ready publication mirrors and portal articles.
- [`prompts/eu/`](prompts/eu/README.md) — complete EU-edition prompts and user guides.
- [`publications/eu-compliance-watch/`](publications/eu-compliance-watch/README.md) — EU Compliance Watch series and publication kit.
- [`publications/civic-forensics-portfolio/`](publications/civic-forensics-portfolio/README.md) — CFP publications and publication kit.
- [`programmes/`](programmes/) — international programme concepts.
- [`research-concepts/`](research-concepts/README.md) — Civic Intelligence Dashboard and other development concepts.

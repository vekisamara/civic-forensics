# Analysis Templates

Use these templates to keep English-language analyses consistent, reviewable and reusable.

- [`analysis-template.md`](analysis-template.md) — standard institutional, legal or public-policy analysis.
- [`quantitative-analysis-template.md`](quantitative-analysis-template.md) — analysis of numerical, comparative, predictive or causal claims.
- [`source-register-template.md`](source-register-template.md) — structured register of documents, datasets and other evidence.

Copy the relevant template into the folder of a new analysis. Do not edit the template file to insert case-specific content.

## Minimum publication checks

Before changing an analysis to `final`, confirm that:

- every material factual claim has a source and precise source location;
- source status and evidence level are recorded;
- facts, legal interpretation, inference and allegation are separated;
- alternative explanations and missing evidence are stated;
- personal and confidential data have been reviewed and redacted where necessary;
- quantitative claims have been tested for numerator, denominator, coverage, comparison and real-world effect;
- the applicable jurisdiction and legal framework are correctly identified;
- a right-of-reply section is included where an institution or identifiable person is materially criticised;
- the conclusion does not exceed the evidence.

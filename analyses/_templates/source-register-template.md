## Source-status legend

- **O — Original:** original physical document or native digital file.
- **C — Confirmed copy:** reliable copy whose identity, completeness and provenance have been checked.
- **R — Reconstructed source:** content known from notes, correspondence, extracts or a secondary record while the original is unavailable.
- **U — Unverified allegation:** information requiring verification.

## Evidence-level legend

- **D1:** primary official evidence;
- **D2:** authentic digital or field evidence;
- **D3:** confirmed secondary evidence;
- **D4:** indirect indicator;
- **D5:** allegation or hypothesis requiring verification.

## Authenticity and preservation notes

Record, where appropriate:

- original filename;
- file format and size;
- date obtained;
- acquisition route;
- metadata reviewed;
- cryptographic hash;
- redactions applied;
- relationship between the original and public copy;
- repository location of the redacted version.

Do not upload restricted originals solely to complete this register.

## Citation rule

R and U sources may guide further research but must not be presented as final quotations or confirmed content. Material public findings should normally be based on O or C sources, unless the unavailability of the original is itself clearly stated as a limitation.

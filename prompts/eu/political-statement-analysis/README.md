# Rapid Forensic Analysis of a Political Statement — EU Electoral Context

**Language:** English  
**Status:** Published methodology  
**Licence:** CC BY 4.0  
**Portal:** https://civicforensics.org

This methodology analyses campaign messages, interviews, speeches, political advertising and media reports connected with European Parliament, national, regional or local elections in the EU.

It distinguishes facts, unsubstantiated claims, value judgements, promises, forecasts and claims about other actors’ intentions. It also tests EU and national competences, political-advertising transparency, targeting, sponsorship, source quality and manipulative or persuasive techniques without treating political disagreement as manipulation.
